# Demo video script — natural narration cut: 1:34

## 0:00–0:11 — Problem and product

Show the Release Witness home screen and begin the judge tour.

“A release result needs a receipt. Release Witness turns a developer's change into bounded risks, runs reviewed browser checks, and preserves evidence a human can assess.”

## 0:11–0:25 — Bounded model role

Show the live Nemotron risk map and expand a check.

“NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, served through Nebius Token Factory, maps the change to every known check. It may prioritize the complete catalog, but it cannot remove coverage or decide a verdict.”

## 0:25–0:43 — Evidence receipt

Show expected versus observed behavior, screenshots and the verified receipt.

“Each check connects the model's risk hypothesis to a reviewed expectation and the exact Chrome observation. The server recomputes the report receipt and every screenshot hash before showing verification. Deterministic browser assertions remain the source of truth.”

## 0:43–1:08 — Ground-truth comparison

Show the baseline failures and candidate comparison.

“The controlled baseline contains two known defects. Release Witness finds both. The candidate resolves both concerns, introduces zero observed regressions, and keeps authentication, payments and other browsers explicitly unverified.”

## 1:08–1:34 — Measured close

Show model provenance and the report action.

“On known ground truth: two of two defects detected and two of two repairs classified as resolved, in under five seconds per model-enabled run. Release Witness. Evidence before confidence.”

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1600 × 900 with browser zoom at 100% and hide notifications and secrets.
- Use saved reports so the take is repeatable and consumes no model calls.
- Verify the final H.264/AAC cut is under three minutes and that repository, demo and video links work in a signed-out browser.
